Gutters that flow all season

Your gutters have one job: move rainwater safely away from your home. In the Northwest, with heavy fall rains and a steady drop of fir needles, they fill faster than most homeowners realize, and a clogged gutter can't do its job.

Northwest Clear Exteriors provides thorough gutter cleaning for homes across Fidalgo Island, Camano Island, Skagit County and Snohomish County. We clear every run by hand, flush the downspouts and test the flow, so your gutters are ready for whatever the season brings.

The hidden cost of clogged gutters

Clogged gutters can cause severe damage over time. When water can't drain, it backs up behind the gutter, rotting the fascia boards and working its way under the edge of your roof into the decking. Overflowing water also spills down your siding and pools at your foundation, which can lead to water in crawl spaces and costly structural repairs.

The weight of wet needles and debris can also pull gutters away from the house. Standing water attracts pests, and moss can spread from clogged gutters onto your roof. A simple cleaning prevents problems that cost far more to fix.

Our complete gutter cleaning process

Hand cleaning

We remove all debris by hand, from loose leaves to packed sediment in corners and seams, so nothing gets pushed into your downspouts.

Downspout flush

Every downspout is flushed to clear hidden clogs and confirm water drains freely.

Flow test

We run water through the whole system and point out any sagging sections or leaks we find.

Full cleanup

All debris is bagged and hauled away, leaving the ground, walkways and landscaping below cleaner than before.
